## Gameplay / collider contract

- `buildFerrisWheel` still owns the wheel: pivot, mount and hinge groups, the 40 cabin
  colliders and the platform carry are untouched; with the zone active it runs with
  `ferrisVisuals: false` (procedural meshes stripped, nothing else) and
  `FerrisHarborRig.setAngle(pivot.rotation.z)` drives the authored wheel and
  counter-rotates the cabin pivots. `FerrisHarborRig.test.ts` checks every cabin pivot
  against the collider formula `hub + (cos a, sin a) * 8.5` at four angles (≤1e-6 m),
  upright orientation at every angle, and that a bullet mark parented to a cabin mesh
  rides along.
- No spawn, HUD, Colyseus, Hunter/Prop rule or anti-cheat change. `harbor-warehouse.json`
  untouched in this pass.
- Runtime `colliderCount` 665 → 703 (38 zone prop colliders: bollards, lamp posts,
  life-ring posts, market tables, crates, reel, kiosk, ticket booth, hub-platform rails).
  Validator confirms none overlaps a procedural collider, leaves the footprint or enters
  the promenade / boardwalk / platform-lane clearance volumes.
- No new route and no unreachable hiding spot: the boardwalk, promenade and the three
  platform lanes stay open; boats are visual (their legacy `boats` zone was also visual).
