## Audit findings that shaped the layout

- The AB strip (x -23.4..29.5, seawall to the Warehouse north wall z -18) has no cinematic zone
  of its own: the Dockside Cafe Bar (procedural `buildDocksideCafeBar` boxes x -9..9, z -43..-29,
  7 m walls, roof deck 7.0..7.3, 2.2 m solid back block, interior spiral stair) sits in the dock
  batches shared with the Mini Mart, six `COL_MOVE_CINE_BAR_*` colliders sealed its two 3 m front
  openings and the west side door, and the legacy east-west road z -25..-19 carries the
  `base_MESH_road_marking` dashes and shared puddles. The Warehouse north wall is
  `COL_MOVE_WALL_BACK` (-23..23 × 0..8 × -18..-17.5); the seawall collider is
  -55..63 × 0..0.9 × -43.45..-42.55 with the `ocean-north` hazard at z ≤ -43.01.
- Ground: island asphalt top y 0.18 (players walk at 0, paint at 0.195), concrete promenade at
  y 0 north of z -38, the bar deck at 0.22 (z -28..-24) with legacy machine boxes, seat plates
  (0.69..0.75) and three bollards (x ±8.5, x 3) — all kept and dressed 1:1.
- Enterability: with the six BAR colliders removed, the procedural shell keeps every wall with
  its openings (front x -5..-2 and 2..5, 5 m lintels; side door z -37..-35, 3.5 m lintel) and
  the legacy furniture boxes (tables, counter, bench, shelving, stair steps) become the
  control-office / workshop fixtures.
